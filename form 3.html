Home Information

Malla Reddy University, Hyderabad (As per Telangana State Private Universities Act No. 13 of 2020, Higher Education (UE) Department dt. 15.6.2020) was established in the year 2020 through the State Legislature Council of Telangana, Govt. of Telangana. It is offering industry-focused specialised Undergraduate and Postgraduate courses with the aim of providing Quality Higher Education on par with International standards. It persistently seeks and adopts innovative methods to improve the quality of higher education on a consistent basis.

Services Information

MRU" can refer to several different institutions. Most commonly, it refers to Manav Rachna University, a private university in India known for its outcome-based education and modern infrastructure. It can also refer to Malla Reddy University, a private university in Hyderabad, India, focused on industry needs. Additionally, Mount Royal University in Canada is sometimes abbreviated as MRU, recognized for its undergraduate focus and small class sizes. Finally, Multi-Disciplinary Research Units are also referred to as MRUs, established by the Department of Health Research, Government of India, to strengthen health research infrastructure.
